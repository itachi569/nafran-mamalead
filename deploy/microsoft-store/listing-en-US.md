# MamaLead — Microsoft Store listing (en-US)

## Product name

MamaLead

## Short description

Build, enrich and manage a local business-lead database from one Windows desktop workspace.

## Description

MamaLead is a local-first desktop workspace for business lead research. It collects business listings through a user-controlled browser session, reads public company websites for contact details, and keeps the resulting database on your own PC.

Use one workspace to:

- collect business listings by keyword and location;
- find public email addresses, phone numbers and social profiles on company websites;
- review suggested decision makers before saving them;
- organise leads into saved lists;
- inspect run history and local usage;
- export selected records to CSV, Excel or branded PDF files.

MamaLead stores leads and exports locally. Online services are used for license verification, purchases, usage limits and optional features that you choose to configure. A Google sign-in is optional and takes place on Google's own page in the app's dedicated browser profile.

Free download. A one-day trial includes up to 300 Maps leads. A Lifetime license is offered at Rs 10,000 as a limited-time launch price. Decision Makers passes cost Rs 1,000 for 1,000 searches or 30 days, whichever comes first. Purchases are processed securely by Stripe outside Microsoft Store. Decision Makers passes do not renew automatically.

## Certification notes

MamaLead is an Electron desktop application. It requires `runFullTrust` to launch its bundled Chromium browser for user-initiated business research and website enrichment, read and write its local SQLite workspace and exports, and open Stripe Checkout in the system browser. It does not install drivers or Windows services and does not request administrator elevation.

For testing, use the trial flow in Settings & Billing → Usage. Enter a tester-controlled email address, retrieve the trial key, and activate it in Account. The 24-hour trial begins at activation and permits up to 300 Maps leads.
