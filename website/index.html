HOW IT WORKS

Scrape, enrich, export

Three steps, all running on your machine. Click a screenshot to open that module.

01

Scrape Google Maps

Enter keywords and a city. The app opens Google Maps in its own browser window, from your Google account if you sign it in, and saves the name, address, phone, website, rating and review count of every place.

Screenshot: Google Maps page, before a run.

02

Enrich from each website

A queue visits every business's own website and pulls out the emails, phone numbers, social profiles and named people it publishes, then writes them to the same lead.

Screenshot: Email Finder and its cascade.

03

Work the list, export

Score and filter the leads, bookmark the ones worth calling into saved lists, and export CSV or Excel files straight to disk.

Screenshot: All Leads, the permanent store.

PRIVACY

Your leads stay on this PC

No cloud, no account, no telemetry. The app has no server to send anything to.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
%APPDATA%\nafran-leads-scraper\leads.db
Stored on disk

Every lead, run and saved list is written to one SQLite file on your machine.

Nothing is uploaded

No cloud sync and no telemetry. The only traffic is the app visiting Google Maps and the websites you enrich.

No account

No sign-up and no login. If you connect Google, you sign in on Google's own page; the password never passes through the app.

Back it up like any file

Copy leads.db somewhere safe and you have a full backup of your data.

HOW A RUN BEHAVES
1.5–3 s between places by default, adjustable up to 10 s. Headless, or a visible browser window you can watch. Runs in the app's own browser, with your own Google account if you sign it in. Stops with a clear message if Google shows a challenge.
