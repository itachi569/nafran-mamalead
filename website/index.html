HOW IT WORKS

Scrape, enrich, export

Scrape and organise leads on your PC. Explore the latest app below; screenshots use an illustrative sample workspace.

01

Scrape Google Maps

Enter keywords and a city. The app opens Google Maps in its own browser window, from your Google account if you sign it in, and saves the name, address, phone, website, rating and review count. Set one total lead limit for the run; it stops at that limit or earlier when fewer results are available.

Screenshot: Google Maps page, before a run.

02

Enrich from each website

A queue visits every business's own website and pulls out the emails, phone numbers, social profiles and named people it publishes, then writes them to the same lead.

Screenshot: Email Finder and its cascade.

03

Work the list, export

Score and filter the leads, bookmark the ones worth calling into saved lists, and export CSV or Excel files straight to disk.

Screenshot: All Leads, the permanent store.

PRIVACY

Your leads stay on this PC

Your leads database stays on your PC. License checks use our server; MamaLead Assist contacts its AI provider when you use it.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
%APPDATA%\nafran-leads-scraper\leads.db
Stored on disk

Every lead, run and saved list is written to one SQLite file on your machine.

What connects online

Activation sends a hashed device ID and OS label to our license server. If you use MamaLead Assist, your message and workspace context go to its AI provider.

Account and licence

Download first, then activate an emailed trial or Lifetime key in Settings & Billing → Account for Maps. A paid Decision Makers pass activates on the purchasing device without a separate key. If you connect Google, its sign-in opens on Google's own page.

Back it up like any file

Copy leads.db somewhere safe and you have a full backup of your data.

HOW A RUN BEHAVES
1.5–3 s between places by default, adjustable up to 10 s. Headless, or a visible browser window you can watch. Runs in the app's own browser, with your own Google account if you sign it in. Stops with a clear message if Google shows a challenge.
