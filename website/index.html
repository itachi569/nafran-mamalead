LATEST APP UPDATES · 1.4.23

Find the right business contacts. Keep every step clear.

Automatic updates, your choice

Turn on background downloads in Account. Get a new-release notice, follow progress and restart when you are ready. Your leads and settings stay on this PC.

Short answers. Straight to the page.

MamaLead Assist keeps replies concise and adds the relevant page name below the answer. Click it to open a module, Reports or App updates.

Read a notification with one click

New scan, export and update notices pop up in the top-right corner. Click to open the related page and mark the notice read, or review it later in Notifications. All/Unread, Mark all read and delete with Undo remain available.

Your Maps limit stays in place

Set one total for the run across keywords and areas. Request 40 and collect at most 40. If fewer results are available, the search finishes with those results.

See progress. Stop when needed.

Social, Email and Phone scans show a live progress panel, the current business, percentage checked and remaining count. Stop scan keeps completed results; failed businesses can be retried.

Your saved work, in one report

Open Insights / Reports for saved lead totals, Maps searches and email, phone and social coverage. Refresh the report or reopen an earlier search.

Live search, no extra click

Type a name, email, phone or profile URL. Related saved matches appear below the search bar; a complete phone number narrows to exact matches.

Your history stays useful

Open earlier finder searches from History, or revisit a whole Maps run with Saved searches. Your search history stays available when you reopen the app.

Choose a website list

Paste links, choose a CSV, TSV or Excel file, or reuse a Maps run. Review ready, duplicate and skipped counts. Scan up to 500 sites at a time and keep the remaining sites ready for the next batch.

One main email. Your choice to verify.

Email Finder shows one main email published on each business website. Choose validity on the page or check the current results later. Follow the checked count and use Stop checking when needed.

Social scans keep moving

Scan several websites at once. A slow or failed site does not hold up the rest; retry only failed sites. Duplicate profiles, hidden spam, share, login, post and video links are left out.

Captured leads stay saved

Maps saves results as they arrive. Reopen interrupted searches with the leads already captured, see stopped-run totals, and keep your keywords, location and Google session choice for the next search.

Results come into view

Website Lookup moves to its results when the scan finishes. Review the published details and source pages, then export the current scan or selected websites.

YOUR EVERYDAY WORKFLOW

A clearer path from a website to a useful contact.

The main number comes first

Phone Finder prioritises the website's main contact number, keeps extra numbers one click away and preserves saved Maps numbers. Targeted checks keep scans responsive, with bounded waits for slow websites.

Read the relevant contact pages

Email Finder keeps one main email published on each business website, including Contact Us pages found through links and sitemaps. Optional validity checks apply to the current list.

Pick the details you need

Website Lookup lets you choose emails, phones, social profiles and addresses separately. When the scan finishes, its results come into view with each site's status and sources.

Exports you can use

PDF and Excel files include the MamaLead logo, clear column headings and properly arranged rows. Export your current finder results, a saved Maps search or selected Website Lookup rows. Email exports keep the same main address shown in the app.

Saved Maps searches, up top

Open an earlier city or keyword run from Saved searches and export just that run. The current scan continues to show its own leads in Results.

Updates with a next step

Click an unread notification card to mark it read. Notifications has All and Unread views, read controls and delete with Undo. Open the related result, revisit a saved Maps run or reveal an exported file in its folder.

MAMALEAD ASSIST

A short answer. The right page.

Get help with the app in English, Urdu, Roman Urdu and other supported languages. Roman Urdu stays in Latin letters, including short follow-ups, rather than switching to Hindi. Ask explicitly if you want a different reply language.

Replies stay concise, with the relevant page-name link directly below the answer. Open Social Profiles, Email Finder, Phone Finder, Reports or App updates with that link. No personal AI-provider key is needed.

Internet access and your plan's AI allowance apply. Assist provides guidance; it does not run app actions for you.

Results depend on public information the website makes accessible. Missing details stay blank; published contact information is not a guarantee of an active mailbox or an answered phone.

PRIVACY

Your leads stay on this PC

Your lead database stays on your PC. Activation and billing use the MamaLead service; optional Assist messages and limited workspace context pass through it to the AI provider.

WHERE YOUR DATA LIVES
%APPDATA%\nafran-leads-scraper\leads.db
Stored on disk

Every lead, run and saved list is written to one SQLite file on your machine.

What connects online

Activation sends a hashed device ID and OS label to our license server. If you use MamaLead Assist, your message and workspace context go to its AI provider.

Account and licence

Choose a trial or Lifetime here, receive your key by email, then download and activate it in Settings & Billing → Account to use the app features. If you connect Google, its sign-in opens on Google's own page.

Back it up like any file

Copy leads.db somewhere safe and you have a full backup of your data.

HOW A RUN BEHAVES
1.5–3 s between places by default, adjustable up to 10 s. Headless, or a visible browser window you can watch. Runs in the app's own browser, with your own Google account if you sign it in. Stops with a clear message if Google shows a challenge.
