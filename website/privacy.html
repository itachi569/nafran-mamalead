PRIVACY POLICY

Your data, explained clearly.

MamaLead is a local-first desktop application. Lead records, saved lists, run history and exports are kept on your computer unless you choose to send information to a third-party feature.

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Information kept on your device

  • Your leads database, lists, run history and exports.
  • Your dedicated Google browser profile and its Google session cookies when you choose to sign in.
  • License installation tokens and API credentials, protected with the operating system's secure storage where supported.

MamaLead does not receive your Google password. Sign-in takes place on Google's page.

Information processed by MamaLead services

  • Email address used to request a trial, buy a license or receive a license key.
  • A one-way hashed device identifier and a short operating-system label for activation and device limits.
  • License, purchase, activation and entitlement records.
  • Maps lead and Decision Makers usage counters needed to enforce plan limits. Decision Makers usage sends a hashed subject reference, not the lead record.
  • Basic service logs needed for security, reliability and abuse prevention.

Payments and email

Card details are entered on Stripe Checkout and are handled by Stripe. MamaLead receives purchase status, customer and transaction references, invoice information and the checkout email needed to deliver access.

Transactional license and trial emails are delivered through Resend. Resend receives the recipient email address and the content of that message.

Optional AI and enrichment services

Nafran Assist is optional and uses the API credential you configure. When you send a message, that message, recent chat messages and the workspace summary shown to the assistant are sent directly from your device to the configured AI provider, which is Cerebras by default.

Optional enrichment providers process only the data you choose to submit under their own terms and privacy policies.

Why we process information

  • Issue and verify trials, licenses and paid passes.
  • Prevent duplicate claims, enforce device and usage limits, and protect the service from abuse.
  • Send purchase, trial and license messages.
  • Provide support and maintain service reliability.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app and remove its application-data folder. Service records are kept for as long as needed to provide the license or pass, meet accounting and security requirements, resolve disputes and prevent duplicate or abusive use.

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information we control. Some transaction or anti-fraud records may be retained where legally or operationally required.

Security

License traffic uses HTTPS. Device identifiers are hashed before transmission, server credentials are not included in the app, and local secrets use operating-system encryption where available. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the sources you access, the information you collect, and compliance with applicable laws and third-party terms. Use of Google services is also subject to Google's Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions or requests, email billing@nafran.co. We may update this policy as the product changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
